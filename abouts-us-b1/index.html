    Supporting charities and the local community

    We are local people, with big hearts, who are passionate about supporting our local communities and charities. We strive to make a positive contribution and have achieved this in a number of ways, including:

    • Playing in Friends Fighting Cancer charity football matches to raise funds for vital local cancer charities.
    • Supporting local schools by organising a ‘Design a Christmas Card’ competition – the winner received theme park tickets, donated by Bernards.
    • Sponsoring charity events, such as bike rides and boxing matches, to raise fund for charities close to the hearts of our team’s friends and families.
