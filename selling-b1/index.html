Selling With Bernards

Bernards’ 6-Step Selling Process

Valuation & Strategy

We begin with a free, in-home valuation to establish the market value of your property. We’ll discuss your objectives and agree on the best selling method and strategy for you.

Property Launch

We launch your property to the market with maximum impact, aiming to capture strong interest early on through a compelling presentation and timing.

Portal & Online Exposure

Your home will feature on all major property portals and on our own site, ensuring visibility to the largest pool of potential buyers.

Enquiries & Viewings

We respond quickly and fully to every enquiry, matching buyers from our database where possible. Viewings are scheduled at times convenient for you, and we gather feedback to help with further marketing.

Offers, Negotiation & Sales Progression

When offers arrive, we qualify them to ensure they're genuine, then negotiate to get you the best price. After agreeing a sale, our Sales Progression team takes over, guiding you through to exchange and completion.

Completion

Finally, on the agreed completion date, once funds have transferred, ownership passes. We’ll coordinate with all parties to ensure a smooth handover of keys and documents.

What we do and how we do it

Professional Marketing

At Bernards Estates, we maximise exposure for every property through a comprehensive marketing approach. From listing on leading portals like Rightmove, Zoopla, and PrimeLocation, to showcasing properties in our illuminated high-street window displays and distinctive boards, we ensure your home stands out. Professional photography, eye-catching videos, bespoke brochures, and targeted online and offline advertising help us reach the widest audience. Combined with proactive communication to buyers and tenants, we create maximum interest and generate quality viewings.

Refer a friend offer

Refer a friend or family member to Bernards Estates and, once their property sale or let is successfully completed, you’ll receive a £100 John Lewis voucher as a thank you. To make it even better, they’ll also receive £100 to spend at John Lewis when their transaction completes.

Community focused

Each of our branches proudly supports a chosen local charity, champions nearby businesses, and whenever possible, works with local suppliers. We’re passionate about the communities we serve and are committed to recruiting locally.

Experienced sales team

Examples of our Video Tours

Mortgages

Our in house mortgage and protection advisors are here to support buyers and sellers offering advice and assistance with mortgage and financial decisions. 

Whether you’re a first time buyer or are already on the property ladder, the team of Vicky, Jamie and  Ayesha are able to offer the most suitable products for your needs and circumstances, from the whole of the market. We also have excellent relationships with preferred conveyancers with considerable experience in dealing with a range of property transactions.

Bernards are members of NAEA PropertyMark and the Ombudsman Scheme, both of which uphold the highest professional standards. We carry professional indemnity and client money protection insurance to ensure our clients are fully protected and have complete peace of mind.
