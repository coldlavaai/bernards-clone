Expert Lettings Services Tailored to You

Guaranteed Rent Service

With Bernards’ Guaranteed Rent, you’ll receive a fixed monthly income on time, every time – even if your property is empty or your tenant misses a payment. No void periods, no hidden fees, and the option to receive up to 12 months’ rent in advance. We also cover full property management and legal support, so you can relax knowing everything is taken care of.

Fully Managed Lettings

Our most popular service for landlords who want a completely hands-off experience. We handle every aspect – from marketing your property and securing tenants to rent collection, regular inspections, maintenance, and compliance checks. With Bernards managing your property, you get peace of mind and more time back in your day.

Rent Collection Service

Ideal for landlords who want to stay involved but cut out the admin. We’ll market your property, secure the tenant, and then take care of collecting rent every month. Payments are processed quickly, and we provide a detailed photographic inventory for full transparency, keeping you protected while you stay in control.

Tenant Find Service

Perfect if you want to manage the tenancy yourself but need professional help getting started. We’ll market your property, source and reference reliable tenants, draw up the tenancy agreement, and secure the deposit and first month’s rent. A simple, efficient way to start your tenancy with confidence.
