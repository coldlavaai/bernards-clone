Land and New Home

Our Service

New Build Consultancy Services

Our land and new homes division works hand in hand with our office network to provide an unrivalled service to the local new build market. We provide a high quality service, expert knowledge and advice to landowners, property developers, investors and private buyers.

Our vast experience and statistics helps provide us with current and detailed market intelligence, enabling us to advise clients on optimising the openings for generating revenue with suitable development sites and land acquisition opportunities of all sizes in and around South Hampshire.

We have the resources to support you through the process of building a new development, from land acquisition and development consultancy to the sale of the new units.

Why Choose us

Land Sales

Together with a selected panel of leading solicitors – we work with major new homes developers to advise and design bespoke packages at competitive rates including drafting of leases and the preparation and advanced approval of sales documentation.

Whether focused on affordable housing, mixed use or complex luxury developments, our services include:

  • Feasibility studies and market research
  • Sourcing land for development
  • Optimising floor areas to maximise revenue
  • Determining suitable finishes, layouts and specifications
  • Off plan buying opportunities
  • Facilitating lettings and management through our extensive network
  • Arranging financial services and mortgage advice for buyers and investors.

If you’d like to discuss a project, please contact Daniel Byrne on 023 9286 4974 or email daniel@bernardsestates.co.uk. You can request that Daniel contacts you by clicking the button below and filling out our short contact form.

Residential Property Investments

We also have the expertise and network to help you buy and sell residential investment property in Portsmouth and the surrounding area. We have formed strong business relationships that give us access to investment opportunities, which include portfolios tenanted blocks, off market and multi-buy new development properties.

Working with our marketing specialists, we devise and execute highly targeted and cost effective marketing strategies. We also understand that analysis and reporting is crucial for clients, and so at the commencement of every project, performance indicators are agreed, and monitored throughout its duration.
