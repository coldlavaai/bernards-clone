    Meet The team

    Bernards has dedicated sales, lettings, and mortgage teams based across our eight High Street offices in Southsea, Portsmouth, Drayton, Waterlooville, Fareham, Gosport, Lee-on-the-Solent, and Havant. Led by Managing Director Daniel Byrne, we’re proud to invest in our people, with many long-standing team members who reflect our supportive, career-driven culture. Whether you’re buying, selling, letting, or looking for mortgage advice, our local experts are here to guide you every step of the way.
