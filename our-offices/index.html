Our Offices

Bernards Estate Agents proudly serves the community through a network of conveniently located offices in Portsmouth, Southsea, Drayton, Fareham, and Waterlooville. Each branch is staffed by experienced, friendly professionals dedicated to delivering exceptional service across residential and commercial property sales, lettings, block management, and financial services. These locations ensure comprehensive coverage and accessibility for clients, fostering strong local connections and deep market knowledge. By combining local expertise with a client-focused approach, Bernards ensures a seamless experience tailored to each customer’s needs.
